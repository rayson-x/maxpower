## Completion

Tickets 01–08 are complete for the current Rust-only feasibility scope. The v7 catalog executes all 24 current exact action×view contexts. The governed report freezes all 54 current records, reports three per-record structural recognition failures, and separates the limited admitted three-source human metric output from quality/equipment evidence that remains unavailable.

## Existing implementation boundary

Existing Canonical packet, subject continuity, RepEngine, RecognitionProfile, EquipmentFusionEngine, LocalMotionCoordinateEstimator, `SealedRep` and the assessment-engine scaffold are reused. Their current existence does not satisfy a ticket unless the ticket's complete external behavior and real-input acceptance criteria pass.

## Data boundary

Current governed personal labels can measure action/view resolution, Rep count and start/end behavior, and negative-window rejection. Phase endpoints, equipment tracks and quality conclusions lack admitted human truth, so Ticket 08 reports their availability, refusal and trace completeness rather than invented accuracy.
