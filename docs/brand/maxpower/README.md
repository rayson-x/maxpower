# MAXPOWER brand direction

## Brand name

- User-facing product name: `MaxPower`
- Visual wordmark: `MAXPOWER`
- Recommended store listing name: `MaxPower — Adaptive Coach`
- Coach persona: `MaxPower Coach`
- Expo slug and npm package name: `maxpower`
- Database names, protocol schema identifiers and the Android application ID are separate compatibility identities; this visual-brand change does not silently rewrite them.

## Primary slogan

- English: **Power, planned.**
- Chinese: **让进步有计划。**

The slogan describes the product's real distinction: training, nutrition and recovery become one adaptive plan. It does not promise a particular physique, speed of progress or medical outcome.

## Alternate campaign lines

- **Stronger, by design.** / **更强，有迹可循。**
- **Train. Adapt. Progress.** / **训练，适应，进步。**
- **Your plan. Evolving.** / **计划随你进化。**

## Icon rationale

The mark is one continuous ascending path that also reads as an `M`. The lower first peak and higher second peak express progressive overload and adaptation rather than instant transformation. Rounded terminals echo motion paths and tracked joints while remaining legible at notification and favicon sizes.

Avoid dumbbells, flexed arms, generic lightning bolts, gradients outside the near-black field, text inside the app icon and unverified performance claims.

## Palette

- Power Black: `#0E100E`
- Motion Lime: `#C6F135`
- Training Paper: `#F7F6F2`
- Safety Terra: `#E85C3A` — reserved for recording, warnings and safety states; not part of the primary logo.

## Naming risk

`MaxPower` is already used by unrelated apps and by a Google Play developer associated with health/fitness content. Treat this direction as a product brand decision, not trademark clearance. Complete legal and store-name checks in target markets before public release.
