# Healthy-adult fitness coach book sources (primary-source verification)

Verified 2026-08-13 from official publisher or professional-organization pages. Scope is healthy-adult fitness coaching; rehabilitation/clinical manuals and consumer bestsellers were excluded. “Year” follows the publisher's displayed copyright/publication year. Priorities assume a practical, evidence-led coach knowledge base rather than a degree-level library.

This is a bounded comprehensive curriculum, not a claim that every exercise-science title should be purchased. A book is included only when it fills a distinct capability needed by an evidence-led coach or by the team maintaining the knowledge base. “Body shaping” is treated as the intersection of hypertrophy, fat loss, body recomposition, body-composition assessment, behavior, and client preference—not as a separate physiology or as spot reduction.

## Curriculum map and acquisition decision

### Phase 1 — mandatory operating spine

1. *ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription*, 12th ed.
2. *NSCA's Essentials of Personal Training*, 3rd ed. — or ACSM's trainer text, not both initially.
3. *Physiology of Sport and Exercise*, 9th ed.
4. *NSCA's Guide to Program Design*, 2nd ed.
5. *Sport Nutrition*, 4th ed.
6. 《中国居民膳食指南（2022）》.
7. 《中国居民膳食营养素参考摄入量（2023版）》.
8. *Motivational Interviewing in Nutrition and Fitness*, 2nd ed.

### Phase 2 — product differentiation for muscle gain, fat loss, and body shape

1. *Science and Development of Muscle Hypertrophy* — wait for the third edition on 2026-10-23 if ingestion is not urgent.
2. *The Science of Long-Term Weight Loss*.
3. *Advanced Fitness Assessment and Exercise Prescription*, 9th ed.
4. *ACSM's Body Composition Assessment*.
5. *Biomechanics of Sport and Exercise*, 4th ed.
6. *Exercise Technique Manual for Resistance Training*, 4th ed.
7. *Motor Learning and Performance*, 7th ed.
8. *The Language of Coaching*.

### Phase 3 — depth and governance

Add recovery, concurrent training, flexibility, strength theory, research methods, measurement, critical appraisal, and selective anatomy only when the corresponding product capability is being built. These books improve the reliability of the system but should not all become coach-runtime prose.

## Fit for a book-to-skill / knowledge-base pipeline

| Content type | Distillation target | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable principles | Canonical concepts and decision rules | overload, specificity, energy balance, measurement error | Keep citations to edition, chapter, and page; record confidence and scope. |
| Programming procedures | Structured templates with inputs, constraints, and review signals | needs analysis, volume/load progression, plateau response | Convert to explicit decisions; do not copy textbook prose. |
| Coaching communication | Prompt patterns and cue-selection heuristics | motivational interviewing, external-focus cues, feedback timing | Preserve autonomy and uncertainty; never present one cue as universally correct. |
| Safety content | Screening, stop, and referral rules | red flags, contraindications, scope boundaries | Keep conservative and independently review before deployment. No diagnosis or rehabilitation plan. |
| Fast-moving claims | Evidence queue, not permanent truth | hypertrophy dose-response, supplements, concurrent-training interference | Recheck against recent systematic reviews, consensus statements, and position stands before promotion. |
| Publisher media | Metadata only | exercise photos, illustrations, video demonstrations, test banks | Do not copy into the knowledge base unless the licence explicitly permits it. |

Book access is a practical constraint: many publisher “ebooks” are locked to HKPropel or VitalSource and are not ordinary PDF/EPUB files. The pipeline must use legally obtained, locally readable source files or the team's own chapter notes; it must not bypass DRM. The resulting private knowledge base should store paraphrased claims, source metadata, and citations rather than reconstructing the books.

## Suggested lean acquisition order

1. Start with the **12th ACSM Guidelines**, **NSCA's Essentials of Personal Training (3rd)**, **Physiology of Sport and Exercise (9th)**, and **NSCA's Guide to Program Design (2nd)**.
2. For a Chinese-facing product, add **《中国居民膳食指南（2022）》** and **《中国居民膳食营养素参考摄入量（2023版）》** before localizing nutrition rules.
3. Add **Sport Nutrition (4th)**, **Science and Development of Muscle Hypertrophy (2nd, or wait for 3rd after 2026-10-23)**, **Advanced Fitness Assessment and Exercise Prescription (9th)**, and **Motivational Interviewing in Nutrition and Fitness (2nd)**.
4. Add the focused body-composition, weight-loss, biomechanics, concurrent-training, recovery, technique, motor-learning, and evidence-governance books according to product capabilities.

## Notes on exclusions and freshness

- Dedicated rehabilitation, corrective-exercise, clinical exercise physiology, pathology, and injury-treatment texts were excluded even when issued by reputable publishers.
- Consumer-oriented “anatomy workout” and bestseller-style books were excluded in favor of academic/professional texts.
- The concurrent-training monograph (2019) and hypertrophy text (2021) remain useful but are the entries most likely to need chapter-level supplementation from recent systematic reviews/position stands.
- Nutrition education must remain within a fitness coach's jurisdictional scope; individualized medical nutrition therapy belongs with a qualified dietitian/clinician.
