# Current Rust motion-understanding capability — 2026-08-15

Status: feasibility/Beta evidence. This is not a production-accuracy or cross-user-generalization claim.

## Result first

Rust catalog v7 contains executable Bundles for all 24 exact action×view contexts represented by the current governed 54-record personal corpus. The governed full-corpus structural replay resolves all 54 records, excludes one record under the frozen governance policy, and executes the remaining 53 through the current Rust lifecycle. All 53 produce a sealed set report with eight dimension findings and conclusion-rooted traces. Fifty records produce at least one non-rejected Rep aligned to a human range under the declared broad structural-alignment policy; three records do not.

This proves that the shared engine and report chain run across every current action family. It does not prove acceptable Rep or movement-quality accuracy.

The current v7 known-video alignment now covers all 53 governance-eligible records: 435 counted predictions against 455 human Rep ranges, 379 candidate matches, precision 87.13%, recall 83.30%, exact-set rate 30.19%, and strict 500 ms / IoU 0.60 boundary alignment on 52.31% of truth Reps. Only 1/53 sets has both exact count and every boundary strictly aligned. This is evaluation-only evidence on the touched personal corpus, not a held-out or cross-user claim.

A separate earlier admitted Rust WASM evaluation covers three sources: 23 confirmed predictions, 23 matches, precision 100%, recall 65.71%, start-boundary MAE 467 ms, end-boundary MAE 498 ms, and exact-set rate 33.33%. It predates catalog v7 and must not be blended with the current result.

## Quality and evidence limits

- Current admitted replay sidecars contain pose but no accepted moving-equipment tracks. Equipment-trajectory accuracy and pose/equipment fusion accuracy cannot be measured.
- There is no admitted human truth for phase endpoints, bilateral timing, support stability, trajectory quality or technique quality. Those dimensions may expose facts or typed abstentions, but no quality-accuracy claim is permitted.
- Normalization remains a causal camera-plane calculation. It does not recover world 3D, gravity, force, muscle activation, injury risk or a unique fatigue cause.
- The current corpus is owner/touched personal data. It cannot establish cross-user population performance.

## Release interpretation

The engine is suitable for a feasibility Demo/Beta that shows Rep results, dimensioned set conclusions, `cannot_judge` states and expandable derivation. The current evidence does not justify calling the recognition or quality judgement reliable for general users. The next evidence sequence is: repair the three structural failures and the per-action boundary/over-count gaps exposed by the v7 report; admit real equipment tracks; add governed phase/equipment/quality truth; then evaluate source-independent and cross-user generalization.
